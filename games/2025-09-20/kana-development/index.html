Kana Development

Kana Development is an interactive webpage built to introduce learners to Japan’s two phonetic writing systems — hiragana (ひらがな) and katakana (カタカナ). The goal of this project isn’t to teach the entire Japanese language or serve as a full vocabulary memorization tool, but rather to provide a starting point for recognizing kana and becoming familiar with how they look and sound.

Learners progress through short, structured lessons that focus on reading and identification. Vocabulary words are included as practice material to reinforce recognition, though memorization is not the primary goal. Some words and audio samples may feel challenging or unfamiliar, and that’s expected as part of the exposure process. For dedicated vocabulary study, tools such as Anki or other flashcard-based systems are highly recommended.

The script displayed on this webpage reflects modern digital Japanese, which can differ slightly from handwritten forms. This project was developed alongside my own study of kana and aims to make the early stages of learning more approachable through an interactive and focused online environment.
